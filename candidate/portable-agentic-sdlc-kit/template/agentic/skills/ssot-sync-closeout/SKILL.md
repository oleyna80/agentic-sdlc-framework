# SSOT sync and closeout

Trigger: approved work is ready to close. Inputs: exact subject and assurance.
Steps: reconcile map/registry only for normative state; retain evidence in reports;
update memory; state residual risk. Optional material process findings belong in
the closeout report, not normative state. Each finding must include an observed
condition, one category from scope/authority/reliability/assurance/evidence
quality, a concrete impact in that category, an evidence/check/decision
reference, and a disposition. Use `none observed` only when there is no material
signal.

Do not record routine status, timestamps, activity logs, agent or model metrics,
raw prompts or transcripts, hidden reasoning, or secrets. Do not mirror findings,
verdicts, or mutable assurance details into maps, registries, plans, or tasklists.
Outputs: truthful closeout proposal. Stop: missing assurance, conflicting SSOT,
no Owner close authority, or a finding that exposes a required scope or authority
change. No authority is granted by this procedure.
